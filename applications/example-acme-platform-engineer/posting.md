# Platform Engineer

**Location:** Remote (US)
**URL:** https://acme-example-co.test/careers/platform-engineer

Acme Example Co is a small, fully-remote fintech company. Our platform
team owns the shared services every product team builds on: the ledger,
the job/queue infrastructure, and the observability stack.

## What you'll do

- Own the reliability of core backend services — ledger, job queue,
  internal APIs.
- Build and improve the tooling other engineers use to ship safely
  (CI/CD, migration tooling, on-call/alerting).
- Debug production issues that span multiple services.
- Pair with product engineers to make the shared platform easier to build
  on, not just easier to maintain.

## What we're looking for

- 5+ years of backend engineering experience, ideally including
  infrastructure or platform work.
- Comfortable owning a service end-to-end — schema design through
  production support.
- Experience improving reliability/observability at an existing company
  (cutting alert noise, reducing latency, migrating brittle batch jobs to
  something more resilient) is a strong plus.
- Proficient in at least one of TypeScript, Python, or Go.

## Nice to have

- Experience mentoring other engineers.
- A track record of shipping developer-facing platform tooling, not just
  product features.
